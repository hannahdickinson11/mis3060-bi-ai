## Reflection

I used Claude Cowork to help me answer the business context questions, compare different prompting strategies, and see how changing a prompt can affect the response. For Part 2, I used Claude to think through Wildcat’s credit risk questions and come up with follow-up questions. For Part 3, I compared zero-shot, role-assigned, and few-shot prompting to see which strategy gave the most useful response.

What surprised me most was how much the wording of the prompt changed the response. Giving Claude a specific role and audience made the answer more practical, while the few-shot prompt helped make the response more organized.

I had to fix some responses because they were too general and did not always connect directly to the business situation. I also had to verify Claude’s claim about debt-to-income ratio in Part 4 instead of assuming the information was correct just because the response sounded accurate.

Next time, I would give Claude more specific business context in my prompts from the beginning. I would also make sure to fact-check important claims as I go rather than waiting until the end.
